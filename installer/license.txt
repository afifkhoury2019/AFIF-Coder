With a local model, AFIF Coder runs entirely on your computer and does not send
your code, files or prompts anywhere; the only network access is downloading the
models you choose, from Hugging Face.

If you connect a cloud model (Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek or another service) with your
own API key, your messages, attached files and the file contents the model reads
are sent to that provider and handled under its terms, and use is billed to your
account with that provider. API keys are stored encrypted for your Windows account.

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. AFIF Coder can
change files and run commands in the project folder you open; changes are shown
for your approval unless you turn approval off. Keep backups of important work.
