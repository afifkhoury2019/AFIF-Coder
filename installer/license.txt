AFIF Coder runs entirely on your computer. It does not send your code, files or
prompts anywhere. The only network access is downloading models you choose in the
Models window, from Hugging Face.

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. AFIF Coder can
change files and run commands in the project folder you open; changes are shown
for your approval unless you turn approval off. Keep backups of important work.
